---
name: campaign-brief
description: Turn a rough campaign idea into a structured creative/media brief. Use when the user describes a campaign concept and wants it turned into something a media buyer or designer could act on.
argument-hint: "[one-line campaign idea]"
---

Turn the idea in $ARGUMENTS into a structured brief with these sections:

1. **Objective** — the single primary metric this campaign is optimizing for.
2. **Audience** — who, described by targeting-relevant traits, not personas.
3. **Offer / angle** — the specific hook, framed the way it will appear in the ad.
4. **Channels** — recommended channel(s) and why, given the objective and audience.
5. **Creative direction** — format (static/video/carousel), tone, and 2-3 concrete
   headline or hook variants to test.
6. **Compliance flags** — anything in the angle that risks policy rejection
   (before/after claims, absolute health/financial claims, targeting exclusions).
7. **Success criteria** — the number that would make this a "keep spending" decision,
   and the number that would make it a "kill" decision.

Keep it to one page. Do not pad sections with generic marketing filler —
every line should be specific enough to hand to a media buyer today.
